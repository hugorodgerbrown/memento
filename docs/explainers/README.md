# Explainers

Visual, one-page explanations of how parts of Memento work, for sharing with people who won't read the code. Open the HTML files in a browser. Each page is self-contained apart from Google Fonts, and it falls back to system fonts offline.

| Page | What it explains | Published copy |
|---|---|---|
| [memento-user-guide.html](memento-user-guide.html) | What Memento is and how you use it, in plain words: a day with it, the words you'll meet (entry, inbox, distiller, client, MCP), what you say to Claude, and where your memories are. Start here. | [claude.ai artifact](https://claude.ai/artifact/KGJUznbPSwjxDHL8ZF9fMc) (private until shared) |
| [memento-under-the-hood.html](memento-under-the-hood.html) | How it's built: the parts and who holds a model, the two ways into storage, the tables and their principles, the nine MCP tools, and what runs on the Mac | [claude.ai artifact](https://claude.ai/artifact/Q2QJk7ob7UbRkc8gDgCwmU) (private until shared) |
| [capture-eval.html](capture-eval.html) | The capture eval: what a case is, how a run works, how it's graded, and the results with and without the skill (24–25 Sep 2026) | [claude.ai artifact](https://claude.ai/artifact/YRU7Myd9mZLoF7uBE71aKi) (private until shared) |

The figures in `capture-eval.html` are a snapshot of the result files in `docs/evals/results/` dated 24 and 25 Sep 2026. Re-running the evals doesn't update the page.

The two overview pages describe Memento running on one Mac (ADR 0020), as of 25 Sep 2026. Update them when that changes, for example when deployment resumes or the morning email is built.
