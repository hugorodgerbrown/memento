Memento overview · user guide

Memento: a user guide

Memento is a private notebook of your life that your AI assistant writes in and reads from. You chat with Claude as you normally would. When you mention something about your life, Claude writes it down in Memento and tells you it did. Weeks later, when you ask "how has my knee been since the summer?", Claude looks it up in Memento and answers from what you actually said, with dates.

In plain words

A day with Memento

You never open Memento or fill anything in. You talk to Claude, or into your Pocket recorder, and Memento is the memory behind the conversation.

  1. 08:10
    You tell Claude: “slept badly, knee sore after padel last night”
    Claude saves two notes, one about sleep and one about your knee, dated today and last night, and replies: “Saved: poor sleep, and a sore knee after padel on 24 Sep.”
  2. 08:11
    You: “actually, don't log the sleep bit”
    Claude removes it straight away. Nothing is ever saved without you being told, and one sentence undoes it.
  3. 13:40
    On a walk, you speak into Pocket: “Decided to stop running until the knee settles.”
    Within 15 minutes, or as soon as your Mac wakes if it's asleep, the recording arrives in Memento's inbox, as your exact words, not yet sorted into notes.
  4. 14:05
    The inbox is sorted. Either the distiller does it on its own, or you ask Claude: “process my Memento inbox”.
    The voice note becomes a note filed as a decision, dated today, and it still points back to the recording it came from.
  5. 3 weeks later
    You ask: “how has my knee been?”
    Claude looks through Memento and answers from your own words: “You mentioned it on 24 Sep (sore after padel) and 25 Sep (you stopped running)…”, and it says so if there's nothing.
Five words you'll meet

What the parts are called

Entry
One saved note: something that happened, a thought, a decision or a reminder. It keeps your exact words, plus a clean one-line version with a real date. Most of Memento is entries.
Inbox
A waiting tray for words that haven't been sorted into entries yet. Pocket voice notes always land here, and so does anything Claude couldn't sort. Nothing in the inbox is lost; it just hasn't been filed.
Distiller
An optional helper that empties the inbox for you. It's a small AI program on your Mac that runs every 15 minutes while the Mac is awake, sorts new inbox notes into entries, and leaves anything it's unsure about for you. It needs its own Anthropic API key. Without it, you ask Claude to process the inbox yourself.
Client
The app you talk to: Claude Desktop, for you. It's the part that understands what you said. Each client has its own key to Memento, and the key says what it may do: Claude Desktop's includes forgetting, so “don't log that” works.
MCP
The plug that lets Claude talk to Memento. It gives Claude nine actions, such as “remember”, “recall” and “forget”. You never use it directly.
How you use it

Things you'll say, and what happens

Say them in your own words; these are examples, not commands.

You sayWhat happens
“Ran 5k in 27 minutes, knee fine”Saved as two entries. Claude tells you what it saved.
“Don't log that”The last save is removed at once.
“Actually it was 60 minutes, not 90”A correction is saved. The old version is kept out of view, not rewritten.
“Remind me to book the physio on Monday”Saved as a reminder for Monday. Nothing notifies you yet: ask “what reminders do I have?” The morning email that will send them isn't built.
“What's in my Memento inbox?” / “Process my inbox”Claude lists what's waiting, or sorts it into entries, asking you about anything unclear.
“How has my sleep been this month?”Claude answers from your entries with dates, and says plainly if there's nothing.
“Summarise my September and keep it”Claude writes a summary and saves it, linked to the entries it came from.
“Forget what I said about my sister”Claude shows you exactly what would be deleted, and deletes it only when you confirm. It can't be undone.

Claude also saves things you mention in passing without being asked, like “exhausted today, but can you help with this email?”, and tells you when it does. It won't save questions, hypotheticals, or other people's private news without asking you first.

Where your memories are

On your Mac, and nowhere else

To see them, ask Claude

There is no Memento app yet. You look through your memories by asking, in any wording: “what did I decide about running?”, “show me everything from last weekend”, “what's waiting in my inbox?”. A read-only timeline page is planned.

To check the raw data, the admin page

At http://127.0.0.1:8000/admin/ on your Mac you can browse entries and inbox notes as stored. It's for checking, not everyday use, and deleting there is switched off on purpose: forgetting goes through Claude, so it's always previewed.

How it's built (the parts, how your words move between them, and the rules they keep) is in Memento overview - under the hood.

For Memento running on one Mac, as of 25 Sep 2026. Setup is in docs/local.md in the repository.
